## Outreach snapshot standard

Each prospect snapshot needs exactly these six decision blocks:

1. **What we observed** — dated public evidence + precise source, including limitations. Never turn a missing public signal into a claim that the business lacks a capability.
2. **Why it might matter commercially** — causal pathway from the public-facing issue to enquiries, bookings, average booking value, repeat visits or utilisation. Say `hypothesis` when appropriate.
3. **Where the value could come from** — targeted revenue lever, mechanism and who controls it (studio/artist), along with a numeric worked example when defensible.
4. **The smallest useful test** — one intervention, bounded duration and clear lead/result measurement.
5. **How success is measured** — baseline, control/comparison, metric definition, data source and attribution method. Record booked deposits, not impressions, as the revenue bridge.
6. **The next action** — offer a free corrected snapshot, 15-minute diagnostic review or priced service only when a real shortcoming has been identified.

## Non-negotiable commercial distinctions

- `observed`: public website/booking-policy evidence.
- `modelled`: explicit what-if assumptions. Potential upside is not "money you are losing".
- `verified`: attributed and reconciled client data over a bounded period.
- Price assumptions must state whether £400 is a completed session value, an average booking, a full project, or a deposit: they are not interchangeable.
- Capacity, incremental variable costs, artist/studio revenue split, cancellations and repeat sessions can substantially alter economics.
- Never multiply by artist count unless the unit of improvement is per artist and genuinely repeatable.
- Do not claim an ROI figure unless intervention price, timing, incremental contribution, capacity and attribution are evidenced.

## Example 1 — Never Say Die! (illustrative only)

**Observation:** Public FAQ invites prospective customers to visit the studio to discuss a booking; artist-specific remote routes may also exist. Verify every route before suggesting a missing online facility.

**Commercial hypothesis:** Prospects who cannot visit may abandon their enquiry; clearer remote enquiry capture *might* recover a share.

**Test:** A 30-day tracked booking enquiry entrypoint linking to artist availability. Record qualified enquiries, first response time, appointment booking and deposit paid. Keep the current alternative booking channels in place.

**Conditional economic illustration:** Assume 80 qualified enquiries/month, baseline 20% convert to paid bookings (16); test conversion 25% (20), producing four incremental bookings/month. At £400 realised average paid booking value, this is £1,600 extra gross revenue in a month or £19,200 annualised if sustained for 12 months. It is **not** a measurement, forecast or verified lost revenue. Verify available chair capacity, causal lift and actual average value.

**Low-friction opener:** "Your studio has a substantial artist team and strong in-person touchpoints. I noticed your FAQ invites people to visit before booking. We could test whether a tracked remote enquiry route converts more of the people who can't visit in person. A four-booking monthly improvement, at an illustrative £400 per booking, would be £1,600 gross revenue; we'd first need to measure your baseline to know whether that is realistic."

## Example 2 — Storyville (illustrative only)

**Observation:** The studio publishes more than one enquiry contact address. Do not imply the team fails to manage those inboxes.

**Commercial hypothesis:** A unified enquiry tracker could make follow-up and conversion attribution easier.

**Test:** 30-day enquiry source and response-time audit, shared follow-up queue, conversion measured to actual deposits.

**Illustrative arithmetic:** One additional £400 booking per week for 46 trading weeks = £18,400 annual gross revenue; at a *modelled* 70% contribution margin, £12,880 contribution before fixed costs. Potential only.

## Example 3 — Off The Rails (verification required)

**Observation:** A directory-listed domain was inaccessible in the research environment. This is **not** enough to claim that customer websites are down.

**Test:** Independent HTTP reachability checks, mobile booking-path review, Google Business Profile link checks and phone enquiry attribution.

**Value:** Do not estimate a monetary loss until an actual broken route is confirmed and traffic/conversion is measured.

## Audit upgrade pathway

The free snapshot earns a response by providing a credible diagnosis hypothesis. The £395 Studio Intelligence Audit becomes the first true client-data baseline, validates the constraint and recommends a 90-day plan. The Primary Constraint Programme implements and measures one intervention. Ongoing monitoring prevents regression and tests adjacent growth levers.

## Scoring

Do not equate a **low public visibility score** with a **high-value commercial opportunity**. Ranking should combine addressable upside, evidence strength, strategic fit, speed, feasibility, capacity and buyer accessibility using the versioned `growth-opportunity-v1` weights. Include a `withheld` state for identity conflicts, unsupported financial claims or contact legality not yet cleared.
