### Finding

INKSIGHTS cannot truthfully rank the three revenue-growth levers until revenue, unique paying customers and completed transactions are observed over a common period.

### Diagnosis

The binding constraint is **measurement readiness**, not a demonstrated weakness in customer count, purchase frequency or average transaction value.

Missing evidence includes:

- period revenue;
- unique paying customers;
- completed transactions;
- qualified leads and new customers;
- repeat behaviour;
- live search observations.

### Three-lever diagnostic

The diagnostic correctly leaves all economic baseline/opportunity fields null rather than back-filling assumptions. Confidence in an economic lever selection is therefore intentionally low and `primary_lever` remains unset.

### Opportunity score

Candidate: **Establish a measurable acquisition and commercial baseline before optimising growth**.

Score: **58.75 / 100** using `growth-opportunity-v1`.

The score is intentionally constrained by a low `impact_score` because economic value has not been measured. Evidence/confidence score highly for the existence of the measurement gap itself.

This lands in the canonical **Backlog / gather evidence** band rather than “Execute”. That is the desired behaviour: a large-sounding modelled number cannot outrank missing evidence.

### Recommendation and decision

Recommendation: collect revenue, unique customers and transactions for a consistent period, add qualified-lead/new-customer and repeat-behaviour data where available, restore live search observations, then rerun INKSIGHTS before prescribing a customer, frequency or ATV growth intervention.

Decision: **approved** as a measurement-first validation intervention.

Intervention status: **in progress**.

## What this proves now

This first run proves that the canonical loop can:

1. consume existing real-business evidence;
2. create a finding and causal diagnosis;
3. run the three-lever diagnostic without inventing values;
4. score an opportunity with deterministic weights;
5. map it into a playbook-backed recommendation;
6. record an explicit decision;
7. create an instrumented intervention with a baseline/evidence trail.

## What it does not prove yet

This is **not yet an uplift case study**. There is no post-intervention observation window, outcome or attribution record. Therefore INKSIGHTS must not claim:

- increased revenue;
- more customers;
- higher purchase frequency;
- higher average transaction value;
- SEO/visibility uplift;
- attributed commercial value.

## Measurement plan

The intervention becomes measurable when the business provides or connects a minimum common-period baseline:

`Revenue = Customers × Purchase Frequency × Average Transaction Value`

Minimum required observed fields:

- revenue;
- unique paying customers;
- completed transactions.

Preferred supporting fields:

- qualified leads;
- new customers;
- repeat customers / transaction history;
- capacity and cancellations/no-shows;
- live search observations.

Once these exist, INKSIGHTS should:

1. reconcile the revenue identity;
2. calculate independent opportunity values for customers, frequency and ATV;
3. rescore the candidate playbooks;
4. choose the first genuine economic intervention;
5. record a baseline window;
6. execute the intervention with business approval;
7. observe the post-intervention metric window;
8. create an `intelligence_outcomes` record;
9. perform attribution and record confounders;
10. update `intelligence_learning` and this case study.
