## Early lessons for the engine

1. **Artist count is changeable.** Source payload counts differed from current named artist rosters for Good Times and SANS PATRIE. Do not mark artist counts VERIFIED without timestamped current source evidence and classification (resident, guest, apprentice).
2. **A manual booking process is not inherently defective.** Good Times explicitly says it does not accept online bookings; the consultation-led journey is intentional. Diagnosis must show impact or risk, not impose one generic booking model.
3. **Missing commercial metrics cannot be inferred.** None of these public websites reveals actual monthly enquiry volume, close rate, client LTV, diary utilisation or profit.
4. **First offer should be a measured diagnostic, not a speculative promise.** Where a studio has a booking form, start by testing whether it records, qualifies and follows up enquiries effectively. A paid audit is appropriate only when the prospective customer recognises the relevant decision value.
5. **Source discrepancies should become reviewable observations** in the existing studio-source table, not silently overwrite candidate payloads. Maintain timestamp, URL, named roster and reviewer decision.
